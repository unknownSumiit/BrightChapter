Fair Housing Statement

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Equal Housing Opportunity

Bright Chapter Realty is committed to compliance with all federal, state, and local fair housing laws. We do not discriminate against any person because of race, color, religion, sex (including sexual orientation and gender identity), handicap or disability, familial status, or national origin, or any other class protected by state or local law.

We will not say, write, or do anything that limits a person’s housing choices based on a protected characteristic. We serve every client with the same level of professionalism and care.

Housing for older persons

Some communities shown on this website may be “housing for older persons” (such as 55+ communities) that meet the requirements of the federal Housing for Older Persons Act. Those communities set their own age eligibility rules. Homes in other communities are available to all qualified buyers regardless of age or familial status.

Learn more or report discrimination

If you believe you have experienced housing discrimination, contact the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) at hud.gov/fairhousing or call 1-800-669-9777.
