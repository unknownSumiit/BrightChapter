12 Home Features That Make Life Easier as You Age

By YOUR NAME · Updated November 24, 2026 · 5 min read

Whether you are buying your next home or planning to stay in your current one, some features make daily life easier, safer, and more comfortable for years to come. Here is what to look for.

Layout

  1. Single-level living: The primary bedroom, a full bathroom, the kitchen, and laundry all on the main floor.
  2. A no-step entry: At least one entrance without stairs, from the garage or a front walkway.
  3. Wide doorways and hallways: Doorways of 36 inches and hallways of 42 inches or more make it easier to move around with a walker or wheelchair.
  4. An open floor plan: Fewer walls mean easier movement and better light.

Bathrooms

  1. A walk-in or curbless shower with a built-in bench.
  2. Grab bars by the toilet and in the shower, installed into wall studs or blocking.
  3. Comfort-height toilets, which are a few inches taller than standard.
  4. Non-slip flooring throughout the bathroom.

Kitchen and everyday comfort

  1. Lever-style handles on doors and faucets, which are easier than knobs for hands with arthritis.
  2. Pull-out shelves and drawers instead of deep lower cabinets.
  3. Good lighting, including bright task lighting in the kitchen and night lights in hallways.
  4. Rocker light switches placed where you can reach them easily.

Outside the home

  • A level, low-maintenance yard, or an HOA that handles landscaping
  • Covered parking close to the entrance
  • Easy access to shopping, health care, and public transportation

Many of these features can be added later. When you search our listings, look for the Single-level filter. You can also read our guide to 55+ communities.
