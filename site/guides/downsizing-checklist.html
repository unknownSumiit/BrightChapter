The Downsizing Checklist: How to Move to a Smaller Home

By YOUR NAME · Updated November 24, 2026 · 6 min read

Moving to a smaller home can free up time, money, and energy. The hardest part is usually deciding what to do with a lifetime of belongings. This checklist breaks it into manageable steps. Start early: three to six months before your move is ideal.

Three to six months before

  • Get the floor plan of your new home, or measure it, so you know which furniture will fit.
  • Start with rooms you use least, such as the attic, basement, garage, or guest room.
  • Work in short sessions of an hour or two. Downsizing is a marathon, not a sprint.
  • Ask family members which items they would like, and set a date for them to collect them.

Sort into four groups

  • Keep: Things you use regularly and love, and that fit in your new home.
  • Give to family: Heirlooms and keepsakes, with a deadline for pickup.
  • Sell: Furniture and valuables through an estate sale company, consignment shop, or online marketplace.
  • Donate or recycle: Charities, libraries, and recycling centers will gladly take much of the rest.

Tips for sentimental items

  • Keep the best few, not all. One or two pieces of a collection can hold the same memories.
  • Photograph items before you let them go, and create a digital album.
  • Scan old photographs and documents so they take up no space at all.
  • Share the stories behind heirlooms with the family members who receive them.

One to two months before

  • Get quotes from at least three licensed movers. Ask if they offer senior move services.
  • Consider a senior move manager, a professional who helps plan, sort, pack, and set up your new home.
  • Schedule utility transfers, mail forwarding, and address changes for banks, Social Security, Medicare, and insurance.
  • Gather important papers, medications, and valuables to move yourself.

Moving week

  • Pack an “open first” box with medications, chargers, toiletries, bedding, a few dishes, and the coffee maker.
  • Label boxes by room and contents.
  • Arrange for help on moving day so you do not do the heavy lifting.
