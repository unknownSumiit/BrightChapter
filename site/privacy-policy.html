Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bright Chapter Realty (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit https://www.yourdomain.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: When you contact us or request a home value review, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), property address (if provided), and your message.
  • Information collected automatically: Our hosting provider and advertising partners may collect your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Settings stored on your device: If you use the Text size button, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage on your device only.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and provide real estate services you request
  • To send you information you ask for, such as homes that match your search
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website
  • To display advertising
  • To comply with legal obligations

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the services you request (for example, with our brokerage, a lender, or a title company you choose to work with), with service providers that help us operate this website, or when required by law. We do not share your phone number with other companies for their marketing.

Phone and email contact

If you give us your phone number or email, we will contact you only about your request. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying “STOP” or emailing hello@yourdomain.com.

Cookies and advertising

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors’ cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, and other states with consumer privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information. To make a request, email hello@yourdomain.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Your browser’s Global Privacy Control signal will be honored where required by law.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law and real estate record-keeping rules.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
